# GitHub Profile Image Guide

Your profile photo is one of the first visual elements hiring managers and recruiters notice. A clean, professional image builds credibility and humanizes your technical profile.

---

## 📸 Key Recommendations for a Professional Headshot

1. **Clear Face Focus:**
   * Frame the photo from the mid-chest or shoulders up.
   * Ensure your face occupies roughly 60–70% of the circular avatar frame.
   * Look directly at the camera with an approachable, confident expression.

2. **Lighting:**
   * Use soft, natural light facing you (e.g., facing a window).
   * Avoid strong backlighting or overhead shadows that obscure facial features.

3. **Background:**
   * Choose a neutral, uncluttered background (solid light grey, off-white, soft muted office/study setting, or a gentle background blur).
   * Avoid busy outdoor scenes, crowds, party settings, or distracting patterns.

4. **Attire:**
   * Professional or smart-casual clothing (e.g., collared shirt, crew neck sweater, blazer, or solid-color tee).
   * Neutral, solid colors (navy, black, grey, white, muted blues) photograph best. Avoid heavy graphics, logos, or distracting text.

5. **Image Specifications:**
   * **Aspect Ratio:** Exact 1:1 square crop.
   * **Resolution:** At least 500x500 pixels (high-resolution PNG or JPEG, under 10 MB).
   * Remember that GitHub crops profile pictures into a circle, so keep key features centered.

6. **Consistency:**
   * Use the same photo across GitHub, LinkedIn, and your technical resume. Consistency across platforms reinforces recognition during recruitment cycles.

---

## 🚫 What to Avoid
* Low-resolution or pixelated images.
* Selfies taken from harsh upward angles.
* Group photos cropped down to just yourself.
* Sunglasses, hats, or accessories that obscure your eyes/face.
* Anime avatars, gaming handles, or meme graphics on professional job-seeking accounts.

---

## How to Update Your Profile Picture on GitHub
1. Navigate to your GitHub profile: [https://github.com/settings/profile](https://github.com/settings/profile)
2. Click **Edit** over your current avatar image.
3. Click **Upload a photo...** and select your formatted image.
4. Adjust the crop tool to center your face within the circle preview.
5. Click **Set new profile picture**.
